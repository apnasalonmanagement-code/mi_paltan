# MI Paltan

**Short description**

Your cricket fan companion for MI updates, membership and exclusive fan experiences.

**Full description**

Welcome to MI Paltan – a destination for cricket fans who want to stay connected with their favourite cricket team.

Explore the MI Paltan experience through a simple and user-friendly mobile application.

**App Features**

- **Membership Plans:** Explore available membership plans and their benefits.
- **Secure Payments:** Complete eligible membership payments through Cashfree's payment gateway.
- **Member Registration:** Create your profile and submit the information required for membership enrollment.
- **Fan Experience:** Access available membership features and app content.
- **Cricket Updates:** Stay connected with cricket-related news and information.
- **Easy Access:** Enjoy a convenient and user-friendly mobile experience.

Download MI Paltan and become part of the fan community!

**Disclaimer:** MI Paltan is an independent fan application and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mumbai Indians or the BCCI unless authorization has been obtained.

# MI Paltan – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 21 November 2026

MI Paltan respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the MI Paltan mobile application.

### 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Mobile number
- Residential address
- City, state and PIN code
- Date of birth
- Guardian information where required for junior memberships
- Membership registration and enrollment details
- Payment transaction references and payment status

Please do not submit information that is not required for the service.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Register and manage customer profiles.
- Process membership applications.
- Manage membership enrollment and verification.
- Communicate important membership-related information.
- Provide customer support.
- Maintain application security and prevent misuse.
- Improve our application and services.

### 3. Payment Information

Membership payments are processed through Cashfree, our payment gateway provider.

Payment credentials such as card details, UPI PINs and banking passwords should be entered only through the payment provider's secure interface.

We may receive transaction references, payment status and related information needed to manage membership orders.

### 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate the application, which may include:

- **Supabase:** For customer profile and membership-related data storage.
- **Cashfree:** For payment processing.
- **Render:** For hosting our membership backend.
- **News API provider:** For retrieving cricket-related news content.

These providers may process information according to their respective privacy policies and service terms.

### 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the application, process payments, provide services, comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

### 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

### 7. Children's Privacy

Parents or legal guardians may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information submitted for a child, subject to applicable legal requirements.
